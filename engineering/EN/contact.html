What the first contact is about

An initial conversation is non-binding. The first step is to understand your technical situation: the existing system or platform architecture, open architecture decisions, integration problems, security boundaries or questions around AI-assisted engineering. Suitable situations also include critical engineering knowledge at risk of being lost, several teams lacking a shared technical view, or extensive requirements and architecture decisions that need structure.

The first conversation is used to clarify the problem, context and useful starting point. This may lead to a clearly bounded assessment, a review or concrete architecture work; if the need is not a fit, that should also become visible early.
