Provision of the Website

This website is static. When the website is accessed, the hosting provider may process technical access data. This may include the IP address, time of access, requested file, transferred data volume, browser type, operating system and referrer URL.

This processing serves the technical provision, stability and security of the website. This access data is not evaluated for marketing or tracking purposes.

Legal Bases

The technical provision of the website is based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. The legitimate interest is the secure, stable and error-free provision of the website.

If you contact me by email, processing is based on Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where the request concerns pre-contractual or contractual communication, or on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR for handling other inquiries. Where statutory retention obligations apply, processing is based on Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR.

Google Analytics is used only with your consent. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and, for storing and accessing information on your device, Section 25(1) of the German TDDDG. You can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future.

Cookies and Web Analytics

Without your consent, this website does not set any cookies. Your choice in the consent banner is kept in your browser's local storage so that the banner does not reappear on every visit.

If you consent, Google Analytics 4 is loaded, a web analytics service provided by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. Google Analytics shows how the website is used, for example which pages are viewed, which page visitors come from and which contact options are clicked (the email address, LinkedIn and links to the contact page). This involves information about your device, browser, screen size, approximate location and time of visit.

For this purpose, Google Analytics sets the cookies _ga and _ga_<ID>, which are stored for up to two years. According to Google, IP addresses of users in the EU are not logged or stored; they are used only to derive the approximate location and are then discarded. Google signals and ad personalization features are disabled. Analytics data is deleted after two months.

A transfer of data to Google LLC in the USA cannot be ruled out. Google LLC is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, for which the European Commission has adopted an adequacy decision.

You can withdraw your consent at any time via "Cookie settings" in the footer of every page. Further information is available in Google's privacy policy.
